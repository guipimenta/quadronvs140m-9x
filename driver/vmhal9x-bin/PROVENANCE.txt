vmhal9x.dll / vmdisp9x.dll / vmhal3d.dll - DirectDraw (and Direct3D DDI 8) HAL
for vmdisp9x-based display drivers. Ring-3 DLLs loaded by DDRAW through the
16-bit driver's DCICOMMAND escape (dddrv.c / control.c); they talk to the VxD
through the FBHDA API (3d_accel.h, API_3DACCEL_VER 20260213 - must match the
VxD exactly, vmhal9x refuses otherwise).
